# Course sources and model ownership

The official [16ESPD site](https://dr14-make.github.io/16ESPD/) and [course repository](https://github.com/dr14-make/16ESPD) specify the exercises. Model sources credit the supplied examples. CSVs and plots come from local simulations.

- **Lecture 4:** DC machine, FieldEMF, converter, battery, assembled drive, regen blend and vehicle/energy benches follow the supplied `HandsOn04` build solutions. They use the given planar vehicle, road load, driver and ABS. They are separate student-package components; they do not replace the original torque-only ElectricMotor. Parameter provenance comments in the reference source are retained.
